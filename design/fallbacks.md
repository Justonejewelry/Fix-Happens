# Fallbacks

## Reduced Transparency Mode
- Increase surface opacity
- Reduce blur to near-zero if needed
- Keep borders and shadows for separation

## Solid Surface Mode
- Replace glass cards with solid dark panels
- Preserve the same spacing and corner radius
- Keep pink accent for active states only

## Low Power Mode
- Disable heavy blur and layered effects
- Simplify gradients
- Favor clarity and responsiveness over visual richness

## Compatibility
- Fallbacks should preserve the same layout structure
- Do not change workflow or navigation behavior
