# Accessibility

## Contrast
- Preserve readable text over glass surfaces
- Use dark backing layers behind translucent panels
- Avoid low-contrast text on busy backgrounds

## Transparency
- Provide a reduced-transparency mode
- Use stronger, more opaque surfaces when needed
- Never rely on blur alone to separate content

## Motion
- Keep motion subtle and short
- Avoid animated blur
- Respect reduced-motion preferences where possible

## Focus
- Visible focus states on all interactive elements
- Keyboard navigation must remain clear in desktop mode

## Fallback
- Solid surface fallback for low-power or accessibility modes
- Maintain layout and hierarchy without relying on glass effects
